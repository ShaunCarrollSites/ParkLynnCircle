Services

What We Do

Walk-in/Roll-in Showers

A walk-in or roll-in shower is one of the most effective ways to improve bathroom safety while maintaining the beauty and value of your home. By eliminating high tub walls and traditional shower curbs, these accessible showers reduce the risk of slips and falls while making bathing easier for seniors, veterans, and individuals with mobility challenges.

To maximize safety and comfort, we can also install strategically placed grab bars, handheld shower wands, built-in seating, non-slip flooring, and other accessibility features. The result is a beautiful, functional bathroom that promotes independence, confidence, and peace of mind for years to come

Full Bathroom & Kitchen Remodels

Whether you’re remodeling for style, functionality, accessibility, or increased home value, ParkLynn Circle creates beautiful bathrooms and kitchens built to fit your lifestyle. From custom cabinetry and premium countertops to luxury walk-in showers, modern fixtures, updated lighting, and durable flooring, every project is designed with quality craftsmanship and attention to detail.

For homeowners planning to age in place or accommodate changing mobility needs, we can seamlessly integrate accessibility features into the design, creating spaces that are safer, more comfortable, and every bit as beautiful as they are functional.

Room Additions

Need more space without moving? ParkLynn Circle designs and builds custom room additions that enhance your home’s comfort, functionality, and value. Whether you’re adding a new bedroom, expanding your living space, creating a luxurious primary suite, building a home office, or constructing an in-law suite for a loved one, we’ll design an addition that seamlessly blends with your existing home.

For homeowners planning to age in place or accommodate changing mobility needs, we can incorporate accessibility features such as wider doorways, barrier-free transitions, accessible bathrooms, and universal design principles that make every space safer and easier to navigate. Every room addition is built with exceptional craftsmanship, thoughtful design, and a commitment to creating spaces your family will enjoy for years to come.

Widening Doorways

Widening interior and exterior doorways can make a home safer, more comfortable, and easier to navigate for individuals who use wheelchairs, walkers, or other mobility aids. By creating wider, more accessible openings, we help reduce tight turns, improve movement between rooms, and make everyday activities easier.

Our doorway widening services can include reframing openings, installing wider doors, adjusting trim and flooring transitions, and incorporating accessible hardware. The result is a more open, functional home that supports independence, aging in place, and long-term comfort without sacrificing the home’s appearance.

Ramps & Lifts

Make entering and exiting your home safer, easier, and more accessible with a custom ramp or mobility lift designed for your unique needs. Whether you use a wheelchair, walker, scooter, or simply want a safer solution for aging in place, ParkLynn Circle provides professionally designed accessibility solutions that improve independence without compromising your home’s appearance.

We install custom concrete, aluminum, and composite wheelchair ramps, as well as vertical platform lifts for homes where a ramp may not be practical due to space, elevation, or property layout. Every solution is carefully designed to provide safe, reliable access while complementing your home’s architecture. From the initial consultation through professional installation, our goal is to help homeowners, veterans, seniors, and individuals with disabilities enjoy greater freedom, confidence, and accessibility every day.
