Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ADU?
+

An Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) is a secondary living space located on the same property as a primary residence. ADUs may be attached to the home, built above a garage, created from an existing space, or constructed as a detached building. They are commonly used as in-law suites, guest houses, caregiver quarters, or private living spaces for family members.

How much does a room addition or ADU cost?
+

The cost depends on the size, design, site conditions, and level of finishes. During your consultation, we'll evaluate your property, discuss your goals, and provide a detailed estimate based on your specific project.

Can you build an accessible in-law suite?
+

Yes. We specialize in designing accessible living spaces that include features such as zero-step entries, wider doorways, accessible bathrooms, and wheelchair-friendly layouts. These additions are ideal for seniors, veterans, and individuals with mobility challenges.

Will you handle permits and inspections?
+

Absolutely. We manage the permitting process, coordinate inspections, and ensure your room addition or ADU complies with local building codes and regulations, giving you peace of mind throughout the project.

What areas do you serve?
+

We proudly design and build room additions and ADUs throughout Pinellas County, including Clearwater, Largo, St. Petersburg, Seminole, Pinellas Park, Dunedin, Palm Harbor, Safety Harbor, Oldsmar, Tarpon Springs, Gulfport, Belleair, Belleair Bluffs, Belleair Beach, Belleair Shore, Indian Rocks Beach, Indian Shores, Redington Beach, North Redington Beach, Redington Shores, Madeira Beach, Treasure Island, St. Pete Beach, South Pasadena, and Kenneth City.
